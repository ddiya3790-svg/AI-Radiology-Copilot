QUESTIONS TO ASK A DOCTOR

A person discussing a possible or confirmed brain tumor with a healthcare professional may want to ask questions such as:

ABOUT THE DIAGNOSIS

- What exactly does my scan show?
- Is there an abnormal area that needs further investigation?
- What additional tests are needed?
- Do I need a biopsy?
- What is the exact type of tumor if a tumor is confirmed?
- What is the tumor grade?
- Where is the tumor located?
- What is its size?
- Should I get a second opinion?

ABOUT TREATMENT

- What treatment options are available?
- What is the standard treatment for this tumor type?
- What are the benefits and risks of each treatment?
- Is surgery necessary?
- Are radiation or medicines options?
- How will we know whether treatment is working?
- What side effects should I expect?
- Are there clinical trials that may be relevant?

ABOUT NEXT STEPS

- What should I do next?
- Which specialist should I see?
- How soon should I have additional testing?
- How often will I need follow-up imaging?
- What symptoms should I report?

ABOUT THE AI RESULT

If an AI system has produced a prediction, useful questions for a healthcare professional may include:

- Can you explain what my MRI shows?
- Does the AI result agree with the clinical interpretation?
- Does this scan require additional imaging or testing?
- What information is still needed before a diagnosis can be made?

The AI system can help a person prepare questions, but it cannot determine the correct diagnosis or treatment plan.
