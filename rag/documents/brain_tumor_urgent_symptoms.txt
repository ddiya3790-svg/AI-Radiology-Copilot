URGENT MEDICAL SYMPTOMS

Some neurological symptoms require urgent medical attention.

Examples include:

- A sudden and extremely severe headache
- A first-time seizure
- A seizure that lasts unusually long
- Loss of consciousness
- Serious injury during a seizure
- Difficulty breathing or failure to regain consciousness after fainting

A person experiencing a medical emergency should seek emergency medical care rather than relying on an AI assistant.

The AI Radiology Copilot should never attempt to manage an emergency through conversation.

For non-emergency but persistent or worsening symptoms, the person should contact an appropriate healthcare professional.

IMPORTANT:

The presence of a symptom does not automatically mean that a person has a brain tumor.

Many symptoms associated with brain tumors can also occur because of other medical conditions.
