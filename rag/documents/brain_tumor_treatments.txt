BRAIN TUMOR TREATMENT

Treatment for a brain tumor depends on many factors.

These may include:

- Tumor type
- Tumor location
- Tumor size
- Tumor grade
- Amount of tumor remaining after surgery
- Whether the tumor has spread
- The person's overall health
- Other individual medical factors

Possible treatment approaches include:

- Active surveillance or monitoring
- Surgery
- Radiation therapy
- Chemotherapy
- Targeted therapy in appropriate situations
- Supportive treatment for symptoms
- Clinical trials for selected patients

Not every person with a brain tumor receives the same treatment.

Some tumors may be monitored with regular examinations and imaging, particularly when immediate treatment is not necessary.

Surgery may be used to remove as much abnormal tissue as safely possible and can also provide tissue for diagnosis.

Radiation therapy uses radiation to treat tumor cells and may be used depending on the tumor type and situation.

Chemotherapy uses medicines to treat cancer cells and may be used for certain tumor types.

Targeted therapies may be appropriate for some tumors depending on their biological characteristics.

Supportive treatment may be used to manage symptoms or side effects.

IMPORTANT:

The AI Radiology Copilot must not recommend a specific treatment for an individual person.

The system should explain general treatment options only.

A qualified healthcare professional should determine the appropriate treatment after reviewing the person's complete clinical information.
