BRAIN TUMOR TYPES
=================

Purpose
-------
This document provides general educational information about the brain-tumor-related classes used by the AI Radiology Copilot image-classification model.

The model has four output classes:

1. Glioma
2. Meningioma
3. No Tumor
4. Pituitary

The descriptions below are educational and do not represent individual patient diagnoses.

--------------------------------------------------
1. GLIOMA
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
----------
Gliomas are tumors that arise from glial cells in the brain or spinal cord.

Gliomas represent a broad group of tumors with different biological behaviors and grades.

General Characteristics
------------------------
Gliomas can occur in different regions of the central nervous system.

Their appearance on MRI can vary depending on tumor type, grade, location, and imaging sequence.

Possible MRI Findings
---------------------
Depending on the tumor and imaging sequence, gliomas may demonstrate features such as:

- abnormal signal intensity
- mass effect
- surrounding edema
- contrast enhancement
- infiltration of nearby brain tissue

These findings are not specific enough to determine the complete diagnosis from an image-classification model alone.

Important Classification Limitation
------------------------------------
The AI model's "glioma" prediction indicates that the input image was classified into the glioma class.

It does not determine:

- glioma grade
- molecular subtype
- exact tumor boundaries
- tumor size
- treatment
- prognosis

Clinical diagnosis requires appropriate medical evaluation and, when necessary, pathological and molecular testing.

--------------------------------------------------
2. MENINGIOMA
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
----------
Meningiomas are tumors that arise from the meninges, the protective layers surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

Many meningiomas are relatively slow-growing, although behavior can vary according to tumor grade and other characteristics.

General Characteristics
------------------------
Meningiomas can occur at different locations around the brain and spinal cord.

On MRI, their appearance can vary according to tumor characteristics and imaging technique.

Possible MRI Findings
---------------------
Depending on the individual lesion, imaging may demonstrate:

- a well-defined mass
- contrast enhancement
- mass effect
- displacement of adjacent structures
- surrounding edema in some cases

These findings require professional radiological interpretation.

Important Classification Limitation
------------------------------------
The AI model's "meningioma" prediction means that the image was classified into the meningioma class by the trained model.

It does not establish:

- tumor grade
- exact location
- tumor size
- growth rate
- treatment
- prognosis

--------------------------------------------------
3. NO TUMOR
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
----------
The "no tumor" class represents images in the project's dataset that are labeled as not containing a brain tumor.

This is a machine-learning dataset label rather than a complete clinical statement about a patient.

AI Classification Meaning
-------------------------
When the model predicts "no tumor", it means that the model found the image more similar to the patterns learned from the no-tumor training examples than to the other classes.

A "no tumor" prediction should not be interpreted as proof that a patient has no neurological disease or no abnormality.

Important Limitation
--------------------
The model may not detect every possible abnormality because it was trained specifically for the classes represented in its dataset.

Therefore, the model should not be used to rule out disease.

--------------------------------------------------
4. PITUITARY
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
----------
The pituitary gland is a small endocrine gland located near the base of the brain.

Pituitary tumors are abnormal growths involving the pituitary gland. Many pituitary tumors are benign.

Hormonal Effects
----------------
Some pituitary tumors can produce excess hormones, while others may not produce hormones.

The clinical effects depend on factors including the tumor and the hormones involved.

MRI and Pituitary Tumors
------------------------
MRI is commonly used to evaluate the pituitary gland and surrounding structures.

Imaging evaluation may consider:

- size
- location
- relationship to nearby structures
- contrast enhancement
- effects on surrounding anatomy

The AI model does not independently measure or determine these features.

Important Classification Limitation
------------------------------------
A "pituitary" prediction means that the image was classified into the pituitary class.

It does not establish:

- tumor size
- hormonal activity
- exact anatomical measurements
- clinical severity
- treatment
- prognosis

--------------------------------------------------
COMPARISON OF THE FOUR AI CLASSES
--------------------------------------------------

Glioma
------
Tumor group associated with glial cells of the central nervous system.

Meningioma
----------
Tumor arising from the meninges surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

No Tumor
--------
Dataset class representing images labeled as not containing a brain tumor.

Pituitary
---------
Tumor class associated with abnormalities involving the pituitary region.

--------------------------------------------------
HOW THE AI MODEL USES THESE CLASSES
--------------------------------------------------

The project's ResNet50 model receives a preprocessed MRI image and produces four probability values corresponding to the four classes.

Example:

glioma:     0.10
meningioma: 0.05
notumor:    0.02
pituitary:  0.83

The predicted class is the class with the highest model probability.

In this example, the predicted class would be pituitary.

The probability values represent model outputs and should not be interpreted as clinical probabilities or certainty about a patient's diagnosis.

--------------------------------------------------
GRAD-CAM EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------

The project uses Grad-CAM to visualize image regions that contributed to the model's predicted class.

Grad-CAM can help users understand which areas influenced the neural network's decision.

However:

- Grad-CAM is not a tumor segmentation method.
- The highlighted region is not necessarily the exact tumor boundary.
- A highlighted region does not prove that a tumor is present there.
- Grad-CAM should not be interpreted as a clinical radiological finding.

--------------------------------------------------
PROJECT SAFETY AND SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------

This AI system is an academic/research prototype.

Its purpose is to demonstrate:

- medical image classification
- deep learning
- explainable AI
- retrieval-augmented generation
- agentic AI workflows

The system is not intended to replace a radiologist or other healthcare professional.

AI predictions must not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis, treatment, or other medical decisions.
